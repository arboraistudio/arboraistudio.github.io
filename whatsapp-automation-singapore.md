## How the Workflow Runs

1. **Customer Starts the Conversation:** A customer sends an enquiry, order request, or appointment question to your WhatsApp business number.
2. **The Workflow Collects Required Details:** Approved questions gather the details your team needs without inventing answers or making policy decisions.
3. **Rules Check the Request:** Missing fields, unusual requests, and restricted topics are flagged before information moves to another system.
4. **Your Team Reviews Exceptions:** A staff member takes over when the request needs judgment, approval, or a personal response.
5. **Approved Data Reaches Your Tools:** The workflow updates your CRM or Google Sheet and keeps the next action visible to the responsible person.
