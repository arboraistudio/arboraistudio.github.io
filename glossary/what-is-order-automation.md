# Order Automation

> Order automation is the use of software to capture, check, and route customer orders with less manual data entry, while people keep control of approvals and exceptions.

- Canonical: https://arboraistudio.com/glossary/what-is-order-automation/
- Updated: 2026-07-18

## Explanation

In many businesses, orders arrive in different formats across email, WhatsApp, PDFs, and spreadsheets. Staff then copy customer, product, quantity, and delivery details into another system by hand, which is slow and prone to mistakes.

Order automation reads the incoming order, extracts the key details as structured data, and checks them against your business rules. Missing or unusual information is flagged early, and the order is prepared for a person to review and approve. Only after approval does the workflow update your CRM, spreadsheet, ERP import file, or order system, and prepare the customer confirmation.

Order automation does not replace ERP, inventory, or accounting software. It sits around the systems you already use and removes the manual work between them. For small and mid-sized teams, the value is faster response, fewer entry mistakes, and more capacity without adding admin headcount.
