Partnership Models

Choose the Collaboration Model That Fits the Opportunity

Commercial terms, margins, and thresholds are agreed privately for each model. Fixed percentages are not published.

Referral

You introduce the opportunity. Arbor handles technical discovery, proposal, implementation, and support under agreed referral terms.

Co-Sell

You manage the relationship while Arbor joins technical discovery, demos, architecture discussions, and proposal development.

Co-Delivery

You lead consulting, transformation, or project management while Arbor owns the technical implementation.

White-Label

Your firm remains the visible provider while Arbor operates as the engineering team behind the engagement.
