How a Human-Reviewed WhatsApp Workflow Runs

The workflow handles repetitive collection and routing. Your team keeps control of exceptions and decisions.

  1. 1

    Customer Starts the Conversation

    A customer sends an enquiry, order request, or appointment question to your WhatsApp business number.

  2. 2

    The Workflow Collects Required Details

    Approved questions gather the details your team needs without inventing answers or making policy decisions.

  3. 3

    Rules Check the Request

    Missing fields, unusual requests, and restricted topics are flagged before information moves to another system.

  4. 4

    Your Team Reviews Exceptions

    A staff member takes over when the request needs judgment, approval, or a personal response.

  5. 5

    Approved Data Reaches Your Tools

    The workflow updates your CRM or Google Sheet and keeps the next action visible to the responsible person.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does WhatsApp automation cost in Singapore?

Arbor's WhatsApp and lead qualification implementation starts at S$3,000 as a one-time setup. The final quote depends on the workflow, integrations, approval rules, and handover requirements. WhatsApp Business Platform and provider fees are separate.

Are WhatsApp Business API fees included in Arbor's price?

No. Arbor's price covers implementation work. Meta charges for eligible delivered messages based on the recipient market and message category, and a solution provider may charge additional fees. Check the current official WhatsApp Business Platform rate card before budgeting usage costs.

Can the workflow update HubSpot or Google Sheets?

Yes. A contained first implementation can send approved lead, appointment, or order details to one CRM or Google Sheet. Arbor confirms the exact fields, access, and error handling during scoping.

Can a staff member take over the WhatsApp conversation?

Yes. Human handoff is part of the workflow. Your team can take over when a customer asks for a person, a rule flags an exception, or the conversation requires judgment or approval.

Does Arbor have an office in Singapore?

No Singapore office is claimed. Arbor's engineering team is based in Dhaka, Bangladesh and delivers remotely for Singapore and international teams with agreed communication and review windows.

How do we start?

Start with one business number, one repeatable workflow, and one destination system. Arbor maps the questions, rules, handoff points, and required fields before confirming a fixed implementation scope.
